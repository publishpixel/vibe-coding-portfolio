GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

Is Northstar a real product?

Northstar is a fictional SaaS concept created as a Publish Pixel portfolio project. Its screens and interactions are demonstrations.

Can a team customize its workflow?

The concept is designed around flexible projects, milestones, owners and team views. A production build could add custom fields, automations and integrations.

Does the demo save data?

Plan selection is a front-end interaction only. No account or payment information is collected.
